# Pay-in-3 KYC and Verification Requirements

Required Identification Documents:
1. Government-issued Photo Identification: Driver's License, State ID, or Passport.
2. Social Security Number (SSN) or Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) for identity verification.
3. Proof of Address: Utility bill or bank statement issued within the last 90 days.
4. Active US Bank Account or Debit Card linked for automated monthly installment deductions.

Verification Process:
Instant digital identity verification takes under 60 seconds during online checkout or inside sales guided sessions.

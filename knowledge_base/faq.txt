# Pay-in-3 EMI Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can students apply for Pay-in-3?
A: Yes! Full-time college and university students aged 18 and older can apply for Pay-in-3 zero-cost EMI. Students without a full-time income can qualify by providing a valid student ID, active bank debit card, and proof of part-time employment, internship stipend, or allowance.

Q: What are the age requirements for Pay-in-3 EMI?
A: Applicants must be between 18 and 65 years old at the time of application.

Q: Is a full credit check required?
A: No, Pay-in-3 performs only a soft credit check during checkout. Soft credit checks do not affect your credit score or appear on hard inquiry credit reports.

Q: What payment methods are accepted for Pay-in-3 installment auto-pay?
A: We accept Visa/Mastercard Debit Cards, Major Credit Cards, and direct Bank ACH Auto-Debit. Prepaid cards are not accepted.

Q: Are there early repayment penalties?
A: No. Customers can clear their remaining 2nd or 3rd installments early at any time without pre-payment penalties or fees.

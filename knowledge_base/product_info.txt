# Pay-in-3 Zero-Cost EMI Product Information Document

Product Name: Fintech Pay-in-3 Zero-Cost EMI
Product Type: Short-Term Consumer Financing / Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)

Overview:
Pay-in-3 Zero-Cost EMI allows eligible customers to split any retail or online transaction into 3 equal monthly payments with 0% interest rate and no hidden charges.

Key Benefits:
- 0% Annual Percentage Rate (APR).
- 3 Equal Installments: 33.33% paid at checkout, 33.33% at 30 days, and 33.34% at 60 days.
- Zero processing fee for order values under $500. Flat $5 processing fee for order values between $500 and $5,000.
- Soft credit check upon application that does NOT impact customer's credit score.
- 3-day grace period for repayment past due date before any late fee ($10 flat fee) is incurred.

# Pay-in-3 Fee Structure & Schedule

## Schedule Breakdown
- **Month 0 (At Checkout)**: 33.33% of order total + Processing Fee (if applicable).
- **Month 1 (+30 Days)**: 33.33% of order total.
- **Month 2 (+60 Days)**: 33.34% of order total.

## Fee Matrix
| Transaction Range | Processing Fee | Interest Rate | Prepayment Fee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $50 - $499 | $0 | 0% APR | $0 |
| $500 - $5,000 | $5 flat | 0% APR | $0 |

## Early Repayment
Customers may clear remaining installments early without any early-pay penalties or fees.

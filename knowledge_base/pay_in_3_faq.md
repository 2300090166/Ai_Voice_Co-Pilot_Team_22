# Pay-in-3 Zero-Cost EMI Product Knowledge Base

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What is Pay-in-3 Zero-Cost EMI?
Pay-in-3 is a short-term financing solution that splits any purchase amount into 3 equal monthly payments.
- Payment 1: 33.33% paid upfront at checkout.
- Payment 2: 33.33% paid automatically in 30 days.
- Payment 3: 33.34% paid automatically in 60 days.

### 2. Is there any interest charged?
No. There is 0% interest charged to the customer. The subvention cost is borne by the partner merchant.

### 3. Are there processing fees?
For purchases under $500, processing fee is $0. For purchases above $500, a standard one-time processing fee of $5 apply.

### 4. What happens if a payment is late?
A grace period of 3 days is provided. If payment is missed past the grace period, a late fee of $10 per instance is charged.
